Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use any of this? expand_more

Not at all. We handle 100% of the setup and configuration. Once everything is live, your job is simple — you get a notification when a new booking comes in and confirm it with one tap. That's it.

How long does it take to get everything set up? expand_more

Most clients are fully live within 2 weeks. The website typically goes live within 5 days. The AI receptionist, booking system, and review automation are set up and tested in parallel so everything launches together.

What if I already have a website? expand_more

No problem. We can either work with your existing site or build you a new one — whichever makes more sense for your business. We'll assess it on the strategy call and give you an honest recommendation.

Will the AI sound robotic when talking to my customers? expand_more

No. We train the AI on your business — your services, your prices, your tone, your FAQs. It responds naturally and professionally. Most customers don't realize they're talking to an AI until a job is already booked.

What happens when a customer asks something the AI can't answer? expand_more

The AI is trained to handle the most common questions and booking requests. If something falls outside its knowledge, it lets the customer know you'll follow up personally — and it notifies you immediately so no lead slips through.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract? expand_more

No long-term contracts. We work month-to-month. We'd rather keep your business by delivering results than lock you into something you're not happy with.

Is this only for big businesses? expand_more

This is built specifically for small independent service businesses. Solo operators and small teams get the most value because they're the ones spending the most time on admin and missing the most leads.

How do I get started? expand_more
